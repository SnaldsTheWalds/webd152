Enjoy Nature In Luxury

Pacific Trails Resort offers a special lodging experience on the California North Coast with panoromic views of the Pacific Ocean. Your stay in Pacific Trails Resort includes a sumptuously appointed yurt, a private deck, and a heated pool. The resort is located near several state parks and beaches, providing ample opportunities for hiking, wildlife viewing, and beachcombing.

  • Relax in our lodge while enjoying our complimentary appetizers and beverages.
  • Savor nightly fine dining with stunning ocean views.
  • Unwind in the heated outdoor pool and whirlpool.
  • Explore the coast on your own or join one of our guided tours.

My favorite beverage is 7Up. I know carbinated sodas are bad for me but I can't stop consuming this lemon limed flavored fizzling water. Ever since I was a child, I've always been a lemon lime soda enthusiast. My grandma would always buy me 7Up and I've loved it ever since. 7Up isn't the only soda I like, there's also Sprite and Starry which have a very similar taste but still have their own quirks that differentiate them. Sadly most stores don't carry 7Up and usually carry Sprite instead since it's significantly more popular. It's fine though since both drinks are very tasty. Anyways, I usually drink 7Up when I'm at my grandma's house. She loves to spoil me with it; keeping a whole stash of it in the cabinet. Why she doesn't keep it in her mini fridge? I have no idea. Overall if you don't mind a mildly sour taste, 7Up is a great choice and I would 100% recommend it to anyone who hasn't tried it yet.
